
# FoodRescue Hub

**Surplus Food Distribution Management System**

*Managing Surplus Food for Community Nourishment*

## Overview

FoodRescue Hub is a surplus food distribution management system designed to help restaurants, bakeries, grocery stores, and catering businesses manage and redistribute usable surplus food to approved recipients and community organizations.

The system aims to reduce food waste by improving food inventory management, donation coordination, pickup scheduling, and donation tracking.

## Problem Statement

Usable surplus food is often wasted because of the following problems:

- **Surplus Goes Unseen:** People and organizations may not know when surplus food is available.
- **Manual Tracking:** It is difficult to monitor available food and identify items that have already been claimed.
- **Wasted Opportunity:** Inefficient coordination can result in usable food being discarded instead of redistributed.

## Target Users

- **Administrator:** Manages and oversees the system.
- **Primary Operators:** Restaurant and bakery staff responsible for recording and releasing surplus food.
- **Stakeholders:** Partner organizations that coordinate and support food donations.
- **End Beneficiaries:** Approved recipients and community organizations that receive donated food.

## Main Features

- User authentication
- Organization management
- Food item records
- Surplus inventory management
- Donation and transfer records
- Recipient records
- Pickup scheduling
- Food status tracking
- Expiration records
- Transaction history
- Monthly donation reports

## Unique Features

### 1. Expiration Priority

Food items approaching their use-by date are prioritized so they can be claimed first.

### 2. Traceability

Each donated item has a record to help track where it was transferred or distributed.

## Donation Lifecycle

The donation process follows these stages:

1. **Available** - The surplus food is listed and ready to be claimed.
2. **Reserved** - A recipient reserves the available food.
3. **Collected** - The recipient collects the food.
4. **Completed** - The donation transaction is completed.

## Project Objectives

- Reduce the waste of usable surplus food.
- Improve surplus food inventory management.
- Simplify the coordination of food donations.
- Organize pickup scheduling and recipient records.
- Track food donation status and transaction history.
- Improve accountability through donation traceability.

## Project Goal

FoodRescue Hub aims to connect surplus food providers with approved recipients through an organized distribution management system, helping ensure that usable food reaches communities instead of being wasted.
